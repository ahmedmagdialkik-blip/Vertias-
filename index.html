About Veritas MUN

Mandate, scholarly rigor, and parliamentary standards

Rigorous inquiry and principled dialogue at the heart of every council chamber.

Veritas MUN is a Model United Nations conference built around careful research and direct debate.

Delegates represent assigned portfolios, draft working papers, and take part in committee sessions.

I

Careful Research

Every delegation enters committee grounded in verified treaty law, historical precedent, and portfolio policy.

II

Direct Debate

Moderated discourse and unmoderated caucuses structured to sharpen clarity of argument and diplomacy.

III

Durable Consensus

Working papers and draft resolutions built through coalition-building, compromise, and accountable leadership.
